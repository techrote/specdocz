# Research study: REPLACE TITLE

**Document class:** Narrative evidence synthesis; not a statistical meta-analysis unless the method demonstrably meets that standard.  
**Reading convention:** [Sxx] identifies an entry in the annotated bibliography. [E] direct evidence, [C] corroboration, [I] inference, [R] proposed engineering recommendation, [U] unresolved.

## Research question, scope and exclusions

State exact question, covered communities/technologies/time periods and what does not count. Distinguish closely related but non-equivalent concepts.

## Method and search provenance

Record search date(s), exact query families, source selection/exclusion principles, evidence quality and coverage limitations. Do not imply exhaustive systematic search when one was not carried out.

## Terminology and historical/background context

Define terms with evidence IDs; attribute historical priority claims and express uncertainty.

## Evidence by source or case study

Tie each assertion to source(s). Distinguish first-party statements, measured results, peer-reviewed interpretation, journalism and community observations.

## Comparative synthesis and counterexamples

Describe common patterns, important differences, selection bias, contradictory results and missing evidence.

## Reusable design and implementation patterns [I/R]

For each: problem, mechanism, prerequisites, applicability boundaries, alternatives, risks and observable acceptance evidence. No invented engine APIs.

## Verification and reproducibility

Specify what constitutes source-matched evidence, tests, runtime matrix, failure cases and provenance. Do not equate visually plausible output with deterministic replay.

## Agent consumption contract

Audit the target repository before adoption. Classify applicable patterns as Adopt / Adapt / Reject / Extend. Pin specdocz commit SHA and paths. Write self-contained project requirements and tests.

## Annotated source register

- **[S01]** Author/organisation, title, publication and date, stable URL, source class, which claim it supports, and limitations.

## Research gaps and maintenance

Open questions, next experiments, what would falsify recommendations, review status and update triggers.
